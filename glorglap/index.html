GLORGLAP Uploader

GLORGLAP Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It runs on the creator's computer and posts the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. Nothing is posted until the creator approves it.

  1. Record. A gameplay session is recorded on the creator's PC.
  2. Cut. The tool finds the loudest, busiest moments and cuts them into short vertical clips with captions, plus one full-length video.
  3. Review. The creator watches each clip and approves or rejects it.
  4. Publish. Approved clips are uploaded with their title and caption: to TikTok through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, to YouTube through the YouTube Data API, and to Instagram through Meta's Graph API.

Own content only

Only the channel's own gameplay is uploaded, to accounts the creator owns.

Minimal permissions

On TikTok it uses user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish, and nothing else.

No viewer data

It never reads followers, comments, messages or anyone else's data.

FAQ

How often are new clips posted?

Whenever there's a new gameplay session, usually several times a week.

Can I use GLORGLAP Uploader for my own channel?

No. It's a private tool for the GLORGLAP channel, and it isn't offered to the public.

Does the uploader collect any data about viewers?

No. See the privacy policy for exactly what it uses.

How do I disconnect the app from a TikTok account?

In TikTok, go to Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions, and remove GLORGLAP Uploader.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

GLORGLAP publishes gameplay videos and clips. This policy covers the "GLORGLAP Uploader" app and this website.

What the app does

GLORGLAP Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the GLORGLAP channel. It uploads the channel's own gameplay videos, titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google and YouTube user data

The app uses YouTube API Services. It requests the youtube.upload permission to upload the channel's videos and thumbnails, and youtube.readonly only to confirm which channel it is connected to. It is used only with the channel owner's own account. It does not collect, store, share or sell any other Google user data, and it does not access any other channel or viewer's data. The sign-in credential is stored only on the owner's private computer and is used for nothing else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Instagram and TikTok

For Instagram (through Meta's Graph API) and TikTok (through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API), the app uses the channel owner's own account only to publish the channel's own clips and to read the account's username. It reads no followers, messages, comments or other users' data.

Visitors and viewers

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and collects no personal information. When you watch GLORGLAP on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Deleting data and revoking access

The app keeps no data about anyone other than the channel owner. The owner can revoke its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites, and in TikTok's settings under Security, Manage app permissions.

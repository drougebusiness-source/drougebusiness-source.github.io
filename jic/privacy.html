Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

JIC Precautions publishes educational preparedness videos. This policy covers the "JIC Precautions Uploader" app and this website.

What the app does

The JIC Precautions Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the JIC Precautions channel. It uploads the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the channel's own YouTube account, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Google user data

The app requests one Google permission, youtube.upload, for the channel owner's own account, and uses it only to upload the channel's videos. It does not read, collect, store, share or sell any other Google user data. The sign-in credential is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private systems and is used for nothing else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Visitors and viewers

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and collects no personal information. When you watch our videos on YouTube, Facebook or Instagram, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
